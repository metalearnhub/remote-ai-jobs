Privacy & disclosure

Who runs this site

Remote AI Work (jobs.dijaedadula.com) is an independent site run by Dijae Dadula in the Philippines. It is not micro1 or any employer, and it is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by them. I don't hire, interview, place or pay anyone. Hiring decisions and pay are made only by the hiring platform.

Referral disclosure

I'm a micro1 referral partner. The "apply" links on this site are my personal referral links, and I may earn a referral bonus if you're hired through one. This doesn't cost you anything and never affects how you're assessed or paid. Role descriptions are summarised from each platform's public posting; pay ranges and openings are shown as posted and can change at any time.

What I collect and why

  • Your email, when you ask for an application link or job alerts. I use it to give you the link, remember your device so the next role opens in one click, and, only if you ticked the box, send you job alerts and my free guide.
  • Basic visit data: which role pages are viewed and clicked, the referring site, your browser type and IP address, to prevent abuse and see which roles are useful.
  • A cookie and local storage entry (rj_lead) on your device that remembers your email for one year. Clear it anytime with "Not you?" on any role page or in your browser settings.

I don't sell or share your email. I don't submit applications or refer anyone on your behalf: you apply yourself on the platform's own site. Your data is stored with Supabase and emails are sent from my own mail account.

Your choices

Every alert email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To see or delete the data I hold about you, email hello@dijaedadula.com. I handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
